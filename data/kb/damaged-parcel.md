title: Damaged parcel
---
If your parcel arrives damaged, broken or wet, keep the packaging and the contents and take photos of both.

Open a ticket in the DAMAGED category within seven days of delivery. Give the tracking number and describe the damage. Our team may ask for the photos and arrange an inspection.

If the damage is visible at the door, you can refuse the delivery. The parcel then goes back to the sender, and you should also open a ticket so we can follow it up.
