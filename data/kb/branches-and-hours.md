title: Branches and opening hours
---
Kervan branches are open on weekdays from 09:00 to 19:00 and on Saturdays from 10:00 to 16:00. They are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

At a branch you can send a parcel, collect a parcel held after failed delivery attempts, or return a parcel.

Bring an identity document when you collect a parcel. Someone else can collect it with a signed authorization and their own identity document.
