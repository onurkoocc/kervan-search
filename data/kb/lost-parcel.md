title: Lost parcel
---
If your parcel has not arrived and tracking shows no new event for five working days, it may be lost.

Open a ticket in the LOST category with the tracking number. We search our branches and transfer hubs for up to ten working days and keep you informed through the ticket.

If the parcel is not found, the sender can claim compensation up to the declared value of the shipment.
