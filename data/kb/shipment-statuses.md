---
id: shipment-statuses
title: What each shipment status means
---
CREATED: the sender has registered the shipment but we have not picked it up yet.

IN_TRANSIT: the parcel is moving between our branches and transfer hubs towards the destination city.

OUT_FOR_DELIVERY: the parcel is on the courier's vehicle today. It can no longer be rescheduled.

DELIVERED: the parcel was handed over at the delivery address. RETURNED: the parcel went back to the sender, for example after repeated failed delivery attempts or a refused delivery.
