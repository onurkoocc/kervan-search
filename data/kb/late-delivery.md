title: My delivery is late
---
Most domestic shipments arrive within one to three working days. Weather, holidays and high season can add a day or two.

If the estimated delivery date has passed and the tracking status has not changed for two working days, open a ticket in the DELAY category with your tracking number. We investigate with the branch and answer within one working day.

A late delivery does not mean the parcel is lost. Parcels are treated as lost only after our search, described in the lost parcel article.
