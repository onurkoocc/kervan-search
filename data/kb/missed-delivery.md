id: missed-delivery
title: Missed delivery and failed attempts
---
If nobody is at the address, the courier leaves a note and the delivery counts as a failed attempt. We try again on the next working day.

After three failed attempts the parcel is kept at the nearest branch for seven days. You can collect it there with an identity document, or reschedule the delivery while the parcel is not yet out for delivery.

Parcels not collected within seven days are returned to the sender.
