title: Opening a support ticket
---
Open a ticket when you need our team to look into a problem. Choose a category: DAMAGED, LOST, DELAY or OTHER, and describe the problem in a few sentences. Add the tracking number when the problem concerns a shipment.

You can open a ticket through the customer agent, on the website or by phone. Each ticket gets a number such as TCK-123456; keep it to follow the ticket.

Do not write passwords or card numbers in a ticket. We answer most tickets within one working day.
