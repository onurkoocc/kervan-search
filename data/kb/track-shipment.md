title: Tracking a shipment
---
Every Kervan shipment has a tracking number: the letters KRV followed by eight digits, for example KRV00012345. You find it on your receipt and in the confirmation message we send when the parcel is handed to us.

To track a shipment, give the tracking number to our customer agent or enter it on the tracking page. You will see the current status, the origin and destination cities, the estimated delivery date and the latest scan event.

Tracking information is updated each time the parcel is scanned at a branch, a transfer hub or by the courier. A new shipment can take a few hours to show its first event.
