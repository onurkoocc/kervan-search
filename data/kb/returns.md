id: returns
title: Returning a parcel
---
To send a parcel back to the sender, bring it unopened, or in its original packaging, to any Kervan branch within fourteen days of delivery, together with the tracking number.

Returns paid by the sender are free for you. Otherwise the return is charged as a new domestic shipment.

You can also refuse a delivery at the door; the parcel is then returned to the sender and its status becomes RETURNED.
