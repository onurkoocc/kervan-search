title: Rescheduling a delivery
---
You can move your delivery to another day if you will not be at home. The new date must be tomorrow at the earliest and at most 14 days ahead. Dates are counted in Istanbul time (Europe/Istanbul).

To reschedule, give the tracking number, the new date and your verification code: the last four digits of the recipient's phone number. You may also tell us the reason.

A shipment that is already out for delivery, delivered or returned cannot be rescheduled. If the courier is already on the way, wait for the delivery attempt or ask for the parcel to be held at the branch after it.
