title: International shipments and customs
---
International shipments pass customs in the destination country. Customs may hold a parcel to check its contents and its declared value.

Duties and taxes are set by customs, not by Kervan. When duties are due, you are asked to pay them before delivery and the parcel waits at the customs warehouse until they are paid.

Customs clearance usually takes two to five working days. Missing invoices or an unclear description of the contents can make it longer.
