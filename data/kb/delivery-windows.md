id: delivery-windows
title: Delivery windows
---
Couriers deliver in three windows: MORNING (09:00 to 12:00), AFTERNOON (12:00 to 17:00) and EVENING (17:00 to 21:00).

The window is assigned when the delivery is planned and is shown together with the delivery date. On the day of delivery you receive a message when the courier starts the route.

Rescheduling changes the delivery date. Requests for a particular time window are noted but cannot be guaranteed.
